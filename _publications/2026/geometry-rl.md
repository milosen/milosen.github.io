abstract: >-
  We present a unified geometric framework in which reward maximization, safe exploration,
  and intrinsic motivation emerge as aspects of a single optimization problem over the
  space of achievable long-term behavior. Classical RL techniques — policy mirror descent,
  natural policy gradient, trust-region methods — extend naturally to nonlinear utilities
  and convex constraints under this perspective, subsuming objectives related to
  robustness, safety, exploration, and diversity.
