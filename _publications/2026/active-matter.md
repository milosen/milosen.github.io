abstract: >-
  We show that physical embodiment in active matter systems enables information processing
  capabilities that exceed what is possible through explicit flow sensing alone. Using
  microswimmers as a model system, we demonstrate that the body itself acts as a
  computational resource, coupling sensory and motor degrees of freedom in ways that
  simplify the control problem.
