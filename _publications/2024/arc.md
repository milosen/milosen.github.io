abstract: >-
  We present the ARC Toolbox, a software tool for generating artificial languages
  with precise control over rhythmic properties. ARC enables researchers to create
  stimuli for studying statistical and rhythmic learning, with parametric control
  over syllable durations, stress patterns, and transitional probabilities.
