abstract: >-
  We present ALPARC, an open-source Python toolbox for generating artificial languages
  with precise control over phonological and acoustic rhythmicity. The toolbox introduces
  a phonological rhythmicity index to quantify confounding periodicity of phonological
  features at the transitional-probability rate, and uses a pseudo-random-walk approach to
  ensure unconfounded stationarity across streams of arbitrary length. ALPARC enables
  researchers to create controlled stimuli for studying statistical and rhythmic learning,
  with parametric control over syllable inventories, durations, stress patterns, and
  transitional probabilities.
